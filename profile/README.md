# Bonafide

**A next-generation identity and encryption platform.**

Bonafide is built on user-sovereign vaults — passwordless authentication, atomic encryption, and privacy by architecture. Your data. Your keys. Your vault.

🔗 **[bonafide.id](https://bonafide.id)**

## What It Is

Bonafide inverts the conventional data model. Institutions hold encrypted tokens. Users hold the keys. Access requires personal authorization, verified through a decentralized trust network.

- **Passwordless Identity** — Device-native authentication rooted in the secure element. No passwords, no tokens, nothing to steal.
- **Atomic Encryption** — Every data unit is independently encrypted. Compromise of one unit reveals nothing about any other.
- **Blind Validation** — Independent validators confirm operation legitimacy without seeing data content.
- **Lease-Based Access** — Institutional access is time-bounded and revocable by design. No persistent keys.
- **PII Elimination** — Institutions never hold real names, emails, or phone numbers. Communication flows through proxy channels under user control.
- **Vault Personas** — Cryptographically unlinkable identities. Plausible deniability is structural, not a feature toggle.

## Where It Fits

Bonafide is sector-agnostic. The same platform protects financial data, health records, identity credentials, and personal communications. Active development is focused on financial services, healthcare, and digital identity infrastructure.
